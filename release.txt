This record, the installer and the download each stand in two places: https://lzrbrwsr.com and
https://github.com/lzrbrwsr/lzrprf. Compare them. A difference means one place does not hold this
release: a copy that was changed, an older answer from a cache, or a release on its way. Fetch both again
before you conclude, and install nothing while they differ.
